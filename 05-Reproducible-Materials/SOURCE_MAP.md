# Source map

The public materials were organized from three Google Drive source folders:

1. 科研补充材料 — A/B code, CIF inputs, C1 practice data, and Npath reproduction evidence.
2. DFT复刻 — production calculation records, external comparisons, attribution, and later validation.
3. DFT复刻的obsidian笔记 — A/B/C1 workflow and learning notes.

Original Drive sources were not modified. Duplicate and historical materials are kept distinct from current evidence. Large proprietary or licensed assets are excluded.
